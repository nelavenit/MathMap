**Intuition.** There is a joke (obviously wrong statement but representing an intuition quite nice):
"The [[Axiom of Choice]] is obviously true, the [[Zermelo's Theorem|Well-Ordering Theorem]] is obviously wrong, and who can tell about [[Zorn's Lemma]]?
—Terry Bona

While we proved AC $\iff$ Zorn's Lemma $\iff$ Zermelo's Theorem.
