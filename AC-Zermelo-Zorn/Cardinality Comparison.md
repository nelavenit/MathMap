**Statement.** For any two sets one is equinumerous to the subset of the other.
$\blacktriangle$ By [[Zermelo's theorem]] we can [[Well-Ordered Set|well-order]] both sets, then by [[Wosets Comparison]] one of them is [[Order Isomorphism|isomorphic]] to the [[Subposet|subposet]] of the other, which includes being bijective to the subset.
