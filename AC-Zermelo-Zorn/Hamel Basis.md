**Definition.** Let $V$ be a vector set over a field $\mathbb{F}$ and $\forall i \in I$ $e_i \in V$. The set $\{ e_i \}_{i \in I}$ is called a <u>Hamel basis</u> if:  
1. $\{ e_i \}_{i \in I}$ is linearly independent, i.e. $\nexists i_1, \dots, i_k \in I, a_1, \dots, a_k \in \mathbb{F}$ s.t. $\sum_{j=1}^k a_j e_{i_j} = \vec{0}$  
2. Every $v \in V$ can be represented as a linear combination of a finite number of elements of $\{ e_i \}_{i \in I}$
