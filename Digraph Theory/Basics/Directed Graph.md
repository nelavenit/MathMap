**Definition.** Let $D$ be a set and $A$ be a [[Relation and Relational Structure|binary relation]] on $D$. Then $(D,A)$ is called a <u>directed graph</u> or simply <u>digraph</u>, if $A$ is [[Irreflexivity|irreflexive]].

**Definitions.** Let $\mathbb{D} = (D, A)$ be a digraph. Elements of $D$ are called <u>vertices</u>. Elements of $A$ are called <u>arcs</u>.

**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{D} = (D, A)$ be a digraph. Vertices $u,v \in D$ are called <u>adjacent</u>, if at least of of the arcs $(u,v), (v,u)$ is in $A$. Relation $D$ is called a <u>adjacency relation</u>.
