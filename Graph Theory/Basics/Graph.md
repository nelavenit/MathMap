**Definition.** Let $G$ be a set, $E \subseteq G \times G$, i.e. $E$ is a binary relation on $G$. Then $(G,E)$ is called a <u>graph</u>, if $E$ is [[Symmetry|symmetric]] and [[Irreflexivity|irreflexive]].
