**Definition.** Let $V$ be a set, $E: V\times V \rightarrow \{0,1\}$, i.e. $E$ is a binary relation on $V$. Then $(V,E)$ is called a <u>graph</u>, if $E$ is [[Symmetry and Antisymmetry|symmetric]] and [[Reflexivity and Irreflexivity|irreflexive]].
