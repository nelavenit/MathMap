**Definition.** Let $G$ be a set, ${\leftrightarrow}\subseteq G \times G$, i.e. $\leftrightarrow$ is a binary relation on $G$. Then $(G,\leftrightarrow)$ is called a <u>graph</u>, if $\leftrightarrow$ is [[Symmetry and Antisymmetry|symmetric]] and [[Irreflexivity|irreflexive]].
