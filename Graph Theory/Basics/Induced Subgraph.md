**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{G} = (G, E)$ be a [[Graph|graph]], $S \subseteq G$. Then a graph $(S, E|_{S \times S}) := (S, E \cap S \times S)$ is called a <u>subgraph of </u>$\mathbb{G}$<u> induced by </u>$S$ and is denoted by $\mathbb{G}|_{S}$, Any subgraph induced by some $S$ is also called simply an <u>induced subgraph of </u>$\mathbb{G}$.
