**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{G} = (G, E)$ be a [[Graph|graph]], it is called <u>chordal</u> if every its [[Cycle and Induced Cycle|induced cycle]] is a [[Cycle and Induced Cycle|triangle]], in other words if it contains no induced cycle of size at least 4. Equivalently, if every cycle in $\mathbb{G}$ (not necessarily induced) of length at lease 4 has a [[Cycle and Induced Cycle|chord]].
