**Definition.** A class of [[Graph|graphs]] $\mathcal{C}$ is called <u>hereditary</u> if every [[Induced Subgraph|induced subgraph]] of any member of $\mathcal C$ belongs to $\mathcal C$ too. In relation theory terms this means $\mathcal C$ is [[Hereditary Class|hereditary relative to ]]the class of all graphs.
