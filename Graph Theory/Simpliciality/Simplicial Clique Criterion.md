**Statement.** Let $\mathbb{G} = (G, E)$ be a [[Graph|graph]], $C \subseteq G$ be a [[Complete Graph (Clique)|clique]] of $\mathbb{G}$. Then $C$ is a [[Simplicial Vertices and Cliques|simplicial clique]] iff it contains a vertex with no [[Neighborhood of Vertex|neighbors]] outside of $C$.
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a simple exercise.
