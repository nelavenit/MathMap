**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{P}=(P,\leq)$ be a [[Partial Order|order]], then $(P,\geq)$ is called <u>dual order</u> (<u>dual ordered set</u>) of $\mathbb{P}$ and is denoted $\mathbb{P}^d$.

**Statement.** For any order $\mathbb{P}$, the $\mathbb{P}^d$ is an order.
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.
