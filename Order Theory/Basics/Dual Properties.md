**Definition.** Properties of [[Partial Order|order]]/order elements/[[Subposet|subposets]]/order subsets that are obtained from each other by reversing all the comparabilities (i.e. considering the [[Dual Order|dual order]]) are called <u>dual properties</u>.

**Examples.**
1. [[Minimal and Maximal Elements|Being maximal and minimal]]
2. [[Least and Greatest Elements|Being a least and a greatest element]]
3. [[Lower and Upper Bounds|Being a lower and an upper bound]]
4. [[Upset and Downset|Being an upset and a downset]]
5. [[Principal Filter and Ideal|Being a principal filter and a principal ideal]] 
6. [[Upward and Downward Directed Subsets|Being an upward and a downward directed subset]]
7. [[Filter and Ideal|Being a filter and an ideal]]
