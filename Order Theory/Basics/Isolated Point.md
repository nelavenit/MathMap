**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ be a [[Partial Order|poset]], then $s \in P$ is called an isolated point iff $s$ is $\leq$-incomparable with all other elements of $P$, i.e. $\nexists p \in P \text{ s.t. } p < s \text{ or } s < p$.

**Statement.** Being an isolated point is [[Self-dual Properties|self-dual]].
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.
