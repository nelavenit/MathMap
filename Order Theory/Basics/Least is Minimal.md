**Statement.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ be an [[Partial Order|order]], then $m \in P$ - [[Least and Greatest Elements|least]] ([[Least and Greatest Elements|greatest]]) implies $m$ - [[Minimal and Maximal Elements|minimal]] ([[Minimal and Maximal Elements|maximal]]).
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.
