**Definition.** 
Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ be an [[Partial Order|order]], $Q ⊆ P$, then $b ∈ P$ is called <u>lower (upper) bound</u> of $Q$, if $∀q ∈ Q$ we have $b ≤ q (b ≥ q)$.

**Statement.** Being a lower bound and being an upper bound are [[Dual Properties|dual properties]].
