**Definition.** Let $P$ be a set and $\leq$ be a binary relation on $P$. Then $\leq$ is called a <u>preorder</u> if $\leq$ is 
1) [[Transitivity |Transitive]]
2) [[Reflexivity|Reflexive]]
**Definition.** The pair $(P, \leq)$ is called <u>preordered set</u> or <u>proset</u>.

**Notation.** We would denote prosets with **Blackboard Bold** letters, e.g. $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq_P),\ \mathbb{Q} = (Q, \leq_Q)$.
