**Definition.** Let $P$ be a set and $\leq$ be a binary relation on $P$. Then $\leq$ is called a <u>preordering</u> if it is 
1) [[Transitivity |Transitive]]
2) [[Reflexivity|Reflexive]]
**Definition.** The pair $(P, \leq)$ is called a <u>preorder</u>.

**Notation.** We would denote preorders with **Blackboard Bold** letters, e.g. $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq_P),\ \mathbb{Q} = (Q, \leq_Q).$

**Remark.** Other common name of a preordering is <u>preorder relation</u>.
**Remark.** Other common names for preorder are <u>preordered set</u> or <u>proset</u>.
