**Definition.** Let $A$ be a set and $\circ$ be a binary relation on $A$. Then $\circ$ is called <u>reflexive</u> if $\forall a \in A$ we have $a \circ a$.

**Definition.** Let $A$ be a set and $\circ$ be a binary relation on $A$. Then $\circ$ is called <u>irreflexive</u> if $\forall a \in A$ we have $a \centernot\circ a$.
