**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ be a [[Partial Order|poset]], $S \subseteq P$. A pair $\mathbb{S} = (S, \leq_S)$ is called a <u>subposet of </u>$\mathbb{P}$ if $\mathbb{S}$ is a poset and ${\leq_S} \subseteq {\leq}|_S$.

**Intuition.** This is a [[Induced Subposet|subposet induced by ]]S, in which we allowed to further remove comparabilities. But we allowed to remove them only in such way that the remaining relation is still an [[Partial Order|ordering]] (i.e. not to break [[Transitivity|transitivity]] and [[Reflexivity|reflexivity]], the [[Antisymmetry|antisymmetry]] can't be broken by removing comparabilities).

2. Every [[Induced Subposet|induced subposet]] is a subposet
