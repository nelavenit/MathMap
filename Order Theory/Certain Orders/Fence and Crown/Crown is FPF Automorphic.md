**Statement.** Every [[Crown|crown]] is [[Fixed-Point-Free Automorphic Order|FPF automorphic]].
$\blacktriangle$ Let $\mathbb{C} = (\{c_1,\ldots,c_n\}, \leq)$ be an n-crown, then $f$ such, that $$
f(c_k):
\begin{cases}
 c_{k+2}, \text{for } k \leq n-2 \\
 c_1, k = n-1 \\
 c_2, k = n
\end{cases}
$$ is an [[Fixed Point and Fixed-Point-Free|FPF]] [[Order Automorphism|automorphism]]. $\boxtimes$

**Intuition.** This, in particular, means crown doesn't have the [[Fixed Point Property|FPP]], even though we get by adding a single comparability to a [[Fence|fence]], and the fences have the FPP.
