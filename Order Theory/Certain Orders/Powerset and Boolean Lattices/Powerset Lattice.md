**Definition.** Let $A$ be a set, then the [[Partial Order|poset]] of the set of all subsets of $A$ ordered by inclusion $(2^A, \subseteq)$ is called a <u>powerset lattice</u>.
**Notation.** $2^A$ is also often denoted by $\mathcal{P}(A)$, where $\mathcal{P}$ stands for the first letter of "Potenzmenge", which is "Power set" in German.
**Notation.** For finite |A| = n, powerset lattice is denoted $B_n$.
 
**Remark.** This order is often called a boolean lattice, but boolean lattice is a more general object.
