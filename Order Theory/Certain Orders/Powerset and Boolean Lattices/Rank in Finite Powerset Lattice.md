**Statement.** Consider [[Powerset Lattice|powerset lattice]] of the *finite* set $X$ — $(\mathcal{P}(X), \subseteq)$. The [[Rank|rank]] of $A \subseteq X$ is exactly $|A|$.
$\blacktriangle$ 
1. $\operatorname{rank}(A) \geq |A|$: there is a [[Linear Order (Chain)|chain]] with $A$ as the [[Least and Greatest Elements|greatest element]], we get it by removing one element of $A$ at a time.
2. $\operatorname{rank}(A) \leq |A|$: the only way for an element to be strictly less than $A$ is to have at least one element less, so we can't have a chain with $A$ as the [[Least and Greatest Elements|greatest element]] the size of more than $|A|
