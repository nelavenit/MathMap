**Definition.** A [[Partial Order|poset]] $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ is called an <u>antichain</u> if every two elements of $P$ are $\leq$-incomparable. Subset $A \subseteq P$ is called an <u>antichain of </u>$\mathbb{P}$ if every two elements of $A$ are $\leq$-incomparable, i.e. if $\mathbb{P}|_{A}$ is an antichain. 

**Notation.** We would also call an antichain the [[Subposet|subposet]] $(A, \leq|_{A\times A})$, which is exactly $(A, =)$.

**Statement.** Being an antichain is [[Self-dual Properties|self-dual]].
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a simple exercise.
