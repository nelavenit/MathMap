**Definition.** A [[Partial Order|poset]] $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ is said to be <u>chain-antichain complete</u> (<u>CAC</u> for short) if every [[Maximal Chain|maximal chain]] in $\mathbb{P}$ intersects each [[Maximal Antichain|maximal antichain]] in $\mathbb{P}$.

**Statement.** In CAC poset every maximal chain intersects every maximal antichain by exactly one element.
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.
