**Statement.** Let $\mathbb{L} = (L, \leq)$ be [[Linear Order (Chain)|linear order]], $l \in L$. Then:
1. $l$ is [[Minimal and Maximal Elements|minimal]] in $\mathbb{L}$ $\iff$ $l$ is [[Least and Greatest Elements|least]] in $\mathbb{L}$
2. $l$ is [[Minimal and Maximal Elements|maximal]] in $\mathbb{L}$ $\iff$ $l$ is [[Least and Greatest Elements|greatest]] in $\mathbb{L}$
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.
