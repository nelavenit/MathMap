**Intuition.** We have a notion of the [[Distance|distance]], so similarly to the diameter of the graph we may ask what's the biggest distance in the poset.

**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ be a [[Partial Order|poset]]. We call a <u>diameter</u> the biggest distance between points of the $\mathbb{P}$. In other words the [[Fence|length]] of the longest [[Fence|fence]] in $\mathbb{P}$. 
**Notation.** We would denote the diameter $\operatorname{diam}(\mathbb{P})$. Let $S \subseteq P$, the diameter of the [[Subposet|subposet]] corresponding to the $S$ would be denoted $\operatorname{diam}_{\mathbb{P}}(S)$, or, if $\mathbb{P}$ is obvious from the context, simply $\operatorname{diam}(S)$.
