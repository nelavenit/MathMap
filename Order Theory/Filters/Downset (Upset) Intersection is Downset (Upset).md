**Statement.** Intersection of any set of [[Partial Order|poset's]] [[Upset and Downset|downsets]] ([[Upset and Downset|upsets]]) is a downset (upset).
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.

**Corollary.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ be a poset, $D \subseteq P$ – downset. Let also $D_1$ be a downset in $(D,\leq|_{D \times D})$. Then $D_1$ is a downset in $\mathbb{P}$ too.
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.
