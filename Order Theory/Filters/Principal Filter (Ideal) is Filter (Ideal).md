**Statement.** Let ($P, \leq$) be a [[Partial Order|poset]], then $\forall p \in P$
1. $\mathord{\uparrow}{p}$ is a [[Filter and Ideal|filter]]
2. $\mathord{\mathord{\downarrow}} p$ is an [[Filter and Ideal|ideal]]

$\blacktriangle$ 
1. The $\mathord{\uparrow}{p}$ is an [[Upset and Downset|upset]] as proven [[Principal Filter (Ideal) is Upset (Downset)|before]] and $\mathord{\uparrow}{a}$ is [[Upward and Downward Directed Subsets|downward directed]] because $\forall p_1, p_2 \in \mathord{\uparrow}{p}$ we have $p$ as their [[Lower and Upper Bounds|lower bound]].
2. Same as (1)
So we actually made a generalization.
