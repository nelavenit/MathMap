**Statement.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq_P)$ be a [[Partial Order|poset]], then for every $p \in P$ the [[Principal Filter and Ideal|principal ideal]] $\mathord{\downarrow} p$ is a [[Upset and Downset|downset]], and a [[Principal Filter and Ideal|principal filter]] $\mathord{\uparrow} p$ is an [[Upset and Downset|upset]].
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.

**Statement.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq_P)$ be a poset, then for every $X \subseteq P$ the $\mathord{\downarrow} X$ is a downset, and $\mathord{\uparrow} X$ is an upset.
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.
