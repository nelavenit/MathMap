**Definition.** Let $(P, \leq)$ be a [[Partial Order|poset]], $p \in P$.  
$\mathord{\downarrow} p := \{ g \in P \mid g \leq p \}$ is called <u>principal ideal</u>,  
$\mathord{\uparrow} p := \{ g \in P \mid g \geq p \}$ is called <u>principal filter</u>.

**Notation.** $\mathord\updownarrow p$ is, by definition, $\mathord{\downarrow}p \bigcup \mathord{\uparrow}p$.

**Statement.** Being a principal filter and being a principal ideal are [[Dual Properties|dual properties]].
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.
