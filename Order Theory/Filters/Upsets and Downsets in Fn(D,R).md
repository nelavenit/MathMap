**Example.** Let D,R be arbitrary sets, consider [[Finite Partial Functions|finite partial functions]] [[Partial Order|order]] $(Fn(D,R),\subseteq)$ and arbitrary $r\in R$, $d\in D$. 
1. $\{ f \in Fn(D,R) \mid d \in dom(f) \}$ is an [[Upset and Downset|upset]] 
2. $\{ f \in Fn(D,R) \mid r \notin ran(f) \}$ is a [[Upset and Downset|downset]]
3. $\{ f \in Fn(D,R) \mid d \notin dom(f) \}$ is a downset
4. $\{ f \in Fn(D,R) \mid r \in ran(f) \}$ is an upset
