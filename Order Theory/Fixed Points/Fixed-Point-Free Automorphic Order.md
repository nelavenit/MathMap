**Definition.** A [[Partial Order|poset]] $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ is called <u>fixed-point-free automorphic</u> if it has a [[Fixed Point and Fixed-Point-Free|fixed-point-free]] [[Order Automorphism|automorphism]].

**Notation.** For brevity we would often write FPF automorphic instead of "fixed-point-free automorphic".

**Remark.** In some literature (e.g. @schroderOrderedSetsIntroduction2016) FPF automorphic is called just <u>automorphic</u>.




**Examples.** 
1. [[Crown]] [[Crown is FPF Automorphic|is]] FPF automorphic
2. [[Crown Tower|Crown tower]] is automorphic
