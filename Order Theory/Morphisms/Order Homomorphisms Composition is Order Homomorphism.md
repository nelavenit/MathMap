**Statement.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq_P), \mathbb{Q} = (Q, \leq_Q), \mathbb{R} = (R, \leq_R)$ be [[Partial Order|posets]]. If $f: P \rightarrow Q$ and $g: Q \rightarrow R$ are [[Order-preserving map (Order Homomorphism)|order-preserving]], then $g ∘ f$ is also order-preserving.
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.

**Statement.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq_P), \mathbb{Q} = (Q, \leq_Q), \mathbb{R} = (R, \leq_R)$ be [[Partial Order|posets]], $\Phi: P \to Q$ and $\Psi: Q \to R$ be [[Order Isomorphism|order-isomorphisms]], then $\Psi \circ \Phi$ is also an order-isomorphism. 
$\color{red}\text{Proof}$ is a trivial exercise.
