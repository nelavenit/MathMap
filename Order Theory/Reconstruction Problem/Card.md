**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{P}$ be a finite [[Partial Order|poset]]. For $p ∈ P$ the [[Subposet|ordered subset]] $P \setminus \{p\}$ with unlabeled elements (we have no way of determining which element of $P \setminus \{p\}$ corresponds to which element of $P$) is called a <u>card</u> of $\mathbb{P}$.
