**Intuition.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ be a [[Partial Order|poset]]. We want for any $p \in P$ to define the [[Induced Subposet|induced subposet]] $\mathbb{P}|_{P \setminus \{p\}}$ with unlabeled elements (we have no way of determining which element of $P \setminus \{p\}$ corresponds to which element of $P$). The way to realize this "unlabeled" is by taking an isomorphism class. 

**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ be a poset. For $p ∈ P$ the isomorphism class of the subposet induced by $P \setminus p$, i.e. $[\mathbb{P}|_{P \setminus \{p\}}]$, is called a <u>card of </u>$\mathbb{P}$.
