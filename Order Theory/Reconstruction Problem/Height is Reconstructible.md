**Statement.** The [[Height|height]] of a finite [[Partial Order|poset]] with at least four elements is [[Reconstructibility|reconstructible]] from the [[Deck|deck]].
$\blacktriangle$ 
1. If the poset is a [[Linear Order and Chain|chain]] than it has the [[Least and Greatest Elements|least element]] (because it's finite) and is reconstructible by [[Poset with Least or Greatest Element is Reconstructible]].
2. Else, height of a poset is a maximal height of the [[Card|cards]]. $\boxtimes$
