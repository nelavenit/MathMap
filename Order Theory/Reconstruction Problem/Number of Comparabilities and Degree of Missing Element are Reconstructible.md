**Statement.** Let $\mathbb{P} = (P, \leq)$ be a [[Partial Order|poset]], then the number of comparabilities in $\mathbb{P}$ (i.e. number of pairs $p_1, p_2 \in P$ s.t. $p_1 \leq p_2$) is [[Reconstructibility|reconstructible]].
$\blacktriangle$ $\#\text{comparabilities} = s(\text{poset of exactly two comparable elements}, \mathbb{P})$, which is reconstructible by [[Kelly Lemma]] (definition of $s(\cdot,\cdot)$ is there too). $\boxtimes$

**Corollary.** For every card $P \setminus \{x\}$ of $\mathbb{P}$, the [[Degree|degree]] of the missing element $x$ can be reconstructed
$\blacktriangle$
1. Reconstruct the number of comparabilities in $\mathbb{P}$: $\#{\text{comp}}(P)$
2. $\deg(x) = \#{\text{comp}}(P) - \#{\text{comp}}(P \setminus \{n\})$ $\boxtimes$
