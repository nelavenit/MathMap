**Statement.** Let $\mathbb{W} = (W, \leq)$ be a [[Well-Ordered Set|woset]]. Then for every two [[Upset and Downset|downsets]] $D_1, D_2 \subseteq W$ we have $D_1 \subset D_2$ or $D_2 \subset D_1$ or $D_1 = D_2$.
$\blacktriangle$ It follows from [[Form of Arbitrary Downset of Woset|the form of arbitrary downset of a woset]].

**Intuition.** In other words any downsets of a woset are $\subseteq$-comparable.
