**Notation.** While considering [[Well-Ordered Set|wosets]] we will denote the [[Upset and Downset|downset]] $\mathord{\downarrow}{x} \setminus \{x\}$ as $[0, x)$ and $\mathord{\downarrow}{x}$ as $[0, x]$.

**Statement.** Let $\mathbb{W} = (W,\leq)$ be a [[Well-Ordered Set|woset]], then every [[Upset and Downset|downset]] $D \subsetneq W$ has a form $[0,w)$ for some $w \in W$.
$\blacktriangle$ Consider $w$ be the [[Least and Greatest Elements|least element]] of $W \setminus D$, the rest is a trivial exercise.

**Remark.** When talking about well-ordered sets the term downset is often called an <u>initial segment</u>.
