**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{W} = (W, \leq)$ be a [[Well-Ordered Set|woset]]. Element $z \in W$ is called a <u>limit element</u> if it has no [[Lower and Upper Covers, Adjacence|lower cover]]. 

**Examples.** 
1. $\mathbb{N} + \mathbb{N}$ has two limit elements: $1_1$ and $1_2$.
2. Every finite woset has exactly one limit element – the [[Least and Greatest Elements|least element]]
