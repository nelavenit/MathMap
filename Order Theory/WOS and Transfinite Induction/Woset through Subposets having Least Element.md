**Statement (Alternative definition of a Well-ordered set).** Let $\mathbb{W} = (W, \leq)$ be a [[Partial Order|poset]]. Then $\mathbb{W}$ is a [[Well-Ordered Set|woset]] iff every [[Subposet|subposet]] $X \subseteq W$ has the [[Least and Greatest Elements|least element]].
$\blacktriangle$ By [[Well-Founded Order|well-foundedness]] of $\mathbb{W}$ any $X \subseteq W$ has a [[Minimal and Maximal Elements|minimal element]], by [[Linear Order (Chain)|linearity]] of $\mathbb{W}$ this element [[Minimal and Least, Maximal and Greatest in Linear Orders|is]] also the least.
