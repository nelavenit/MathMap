**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{A} = (A, (R_i)_{i \in I}$ be a [[Relation and Relational Structure|relational structure]]. Then <u>Gaifman Graph of </u>$\mathbb{A}$, denoted as $\operatorname{Gf}(\mathbb{A})$, is a [[Graph|graph]] $(A, E)$ with $(a_1, a_2) \in E$ iff some tuple in some $R_i$ contains both $a_1$ and $a_2$.

**Examples.**
1. $\operatorname{Gf}()$ is identity on graphs
2. Gaifman graph of a [[Poset|poset]] is its [[Comparability Graph|comparability graph]]
