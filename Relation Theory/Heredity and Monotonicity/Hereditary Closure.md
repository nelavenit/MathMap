**Definition.** Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a class of [[Relation and Relational Structure|relational structures]], we call $\mathcal{K}$ it's <u>hereditary closure</u> if:
1. $\mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathcal{K}$
2. $\mathcal K$ is [[Hereditary Class|hereditary]]
3. There is no smaller hereditary class $\mathcal{I}$ such that $\mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathcal{I} \subseteq \mathcal{K}$
