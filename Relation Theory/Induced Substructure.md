**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{A} = (A, (R_i)_{i \in I})$ be a [[Relation and Relational Structure|relational structure]], $S \subseteq A$. A relational structure $(S, (R_i|_{S^{n_i}})_{i \in I}))$ is called a <u>substructure of </u>$\mathbb{A}$<u> induced by </u>$S$ and is denoted by $\mathbb{A}|_{S}$. Any substructure induced by some $S$ is also called simply an <u>induced substructure of </u>$\mathbb{A}$.

**Examples.** 
1. [[Induced Subposet]] is an induced substructure of a [[Poset|poset]]
2. [[Induced Subgraph]] is an induced substructure of a [[Graph|graph]]

**Remark.** Unlike [[Substructure|substructure]], "induced" restricts to remove tuples from any $R_i|_{S^{n_i}}$.
