**Definition.** Let $\mathbb{A} = (A, (R^{\mathbb{A}}_i)_{i \in I}), \mathbb{B} = (B, (R_i^{\mathbb{B}})_{i \in I})$ be [[Relation and Relational Structure|relational structures]] with the same [[Relation and Relational Structure|signature]]. Then $\mathbb{B}$ is called a <u>substructure of </u>$\mathbb{A}$ if $A \subseteq B$ and $R_i^{\mathbb{B}} \subseteq R_i^{\mathbb{A}}$ for every $i \in I$.

1. [[Subgraph]] is a substructure of a [[Graph|graph]]

3. Every [[Induced Substructure|induced substructure]] is a substructure

**Remark.** In some sources $\mathbb{B}$ is called a <u>week substructure of </u>$\mathbb{A}$ to emphasize the difference from the [[Induced Substructure|induced substructure]], because induced substructure is often called simply "substructure".
