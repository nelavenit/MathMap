**Definition.** Let $A$ be a set and $\mathrel{R}$ be a [[Relation and Relational Structure|binary relation]] on $A$. Then $\mathrel{R}$ is called <u>transitive</u>
if $\forall a_1, a_2, a_3 \in A$ we have $a_1 \mathrel{R} a_2$ and $a_2 \mathrel{R} a_3$ imply $a_1 \mathrel{R} a_3$.
